Avon is a university-grade platform for computer science courses. It keeps submissions, autograding, rubrics, feedback, and LMS handoff in one place so lecturers spend less time on tooling and more time teaching.

## Why we built it

Most courses stitch together a Git forge, a CI pipeline, a spreadsheet, and an LMS. That works until the cohort grows, teams change, or grades need to sync on a deadline.

Avon is designed around the full loop:

1. **Provision** — starter code to student or team repositories
2. **Test** — autograder feedback on every submission
3. **Suggest** — hints and guidance where they help learning
4. **Assess** — rubrics, grades, and LMS handoff

## What is available today

You can explore product pages for Provision, Test, Suggest, and Assess on this site. Documentation and deeper integration guides will land here as they ship.
